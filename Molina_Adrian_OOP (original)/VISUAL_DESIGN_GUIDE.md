## ? RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR

### On Small Screens
- Card remains centered
- Card size fixed (won't shrink below minimum)
- ScrollBar appears if needed (RegisterForm)
- All controls remain proportional

### On Large Screens
- Card stays centered
- Extra space around card
- No stretching or distortion
- Professional centered appearance

### On Window Resize
- CenterCardPanel() called
- Card re-positions to center
- No flickering or jumping
- Smooth repositioning
